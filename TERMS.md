## Access and content

You choose the BookStack instance, credentials, connected clients, and deployment
configuration. Use credentials and content you are authorized to access, and
review AI-generated changes before relying on them. Write operations are disabled
by default; enabling them permits tools to create, modify, and delete content
within the configured BookStack user's permissions. Maintain backups appropriate
to your deployment.

The project does not claim ownership of your wiki content. Data handling is
described in [PRIVACY.md](./PRIVACY.md), and deployment security guidance and
vulnerability reporting are described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
